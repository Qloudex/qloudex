# Qloudex roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not delivery guarantees. Priorities can change
as technical findings and customer needs become clearer. Completed work should
be documented in the relevant repository rather than left here as a plan.

## Now

Focus: documentation quality, reproducible demonstrations, and service
readiness.

- Keep the website, organization profile, project hub, and project READMEs
  factually aligned.
- Document verified setup steps, architectures, limitations, security
  considerations, and license status for each engineering project.
- Improve reproducible local demonstrations without presenting them as customer
  deployments.
- Define practical discovery, scoping, validation, and handover templates for
  the three service offerings.
- Confirm and test the public project-contact channel before treating it as an
  operational lead inbox.

## Next

Focus: stronger technical projects, repeatable delivery, and evidence from real
feedback.

- Prioritize project improvements based on reproducible gaps rather than adding
  unsupported feature breadth.
- Strengthen tests, dependency maintenance, security guidance, and release
  practices in the individual repositories.
- Correct environment-specific or stale examples where they prevent a
  documented workflow from being reproduced.
- Turn recurring delivery steps into reusable internal checklists and
  templates.
- Gather customer and contributor feedback, with permission and without
  publishing confidential information.
- Use validated feedback to decide which service workflows deserve deeper
  investment.

## Later

Focus: potential products based on repeated, validated customer needs.

- Evaluate whether recurring AI workflow, cloud operations, or integration
  problems justify a focused SaaS product.
- Prototype only after a clear user, problem, workflow, and adoption path have
  been identified.
- Define security, privacy, support, pricing, and operational requirements
  before offering a subscription.
- Keep experimental product work clearly labeled until it is genuinely
  available.

No Qloudex SaaS subscription is currently represented by this roadmap as
launched or available for sale.
